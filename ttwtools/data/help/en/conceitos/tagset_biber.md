Biber (1988) described dozens of [**lexico-grammatical features** (tenses, pronouns, subordination, passives, modals...) that co-vary across registers]{marca amarelo}. BiberPlus tags these features and adds basic word classes and social media markers.

[Categories go from **A** (tense and aspect) to **P** (negation)]{marca amarelo}. See the full list with `ttwtools tagset biber` or in the "see tags" panel.

## How to read
- [Each feature becomes a count column per text; normalised per 1,000 words, it is used by KFA]{marca verde}.
- [Features in the same category usually tell a similar story]{marca verde} (e.g. many 1st and 2nd person pronouns = a more interactive register).

## Caveats
- [Tagging is automatic: check in the KWIC a few examples of the features your interpretation relies on]{marca rosa}.
- [Some features are rare and unstable in short texts]{marca rosa}.
